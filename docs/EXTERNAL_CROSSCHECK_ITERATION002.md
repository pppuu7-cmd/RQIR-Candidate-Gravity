## 1. Bosonic Gaussian channel dilation

F. Caruso, J. Eisert, V. Giovannetti, A. S. Holevo, *Multi-mode bosonic Gaussian channels*, arXiv:0804.0511.

F. Caruso, J. Eisert, V. Giovannetti, A. S. Holevo, *The optimal unitary dilation for bosonic Gaussian channels*, arXiv:1009.1108.

Cross-check relevance:

- general multi-mode bosonic Gaussian channels admit unitary Gaussian dilations with environmental modes;
- explicit pure-environment/Stinespring dilations are available;
- therefore an input-output Gaussian channel law and a quantum-limited CP floor are not, on their own, evidence for a new microscopic gravity law.

This supports `RQIR-CG-NG-001` and `ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC/FOUNDATIONAL_DERIVATION_002.md`.

## 2. Stochastic gravity / influence functional

B. L. Hu, E. Verdaguer, *Stochastic Gravity: A Primer with Applications*, arXiv:gr-qc/0211090.

B. L. Hu, E. Verdaguer, *Stochastic Gravity: Theory and Applications*, arXiv:gr-qc/0307032 (and later Living Reviews version).

Cross-check relevance:

- influence-functional / Schwinger-Keldysh methods naturally expose dissipation/response, fluctuations/noise and decoherence together;
- a nonzero noise kernel is not by itself a proof of intrinsic quantum geometry;
- response plus noise can already arise within controlled matter/gravity open-system descriptions.

This supports the decision not to use generic `N` or `D/chi^R` as a new-gravity discriminator.

## 3. General relativity as low-energy quantum EFT

J. F. Donoghue, *General relativity as an effective field theory: The leading quantum corrections*, arXiv:gr-qc/9405057.

J. F. Donoghue, *Quantum General Relativity and Effective Field Theory*, arXiv:2211.09902.

Cross-check relevance:

- GR can be treated as a controlled quantum EFT at ordinary/low energies;
- low-energy quantum corrections and graviton propagation are meaningful without a UV completion;
- therefore a metric-only local minimum-complexity quantum completion naturally has a strong C5 comparator and cannot be called novel merely because gravitational perturbations are quantized.

This supports `CONDITIONAL_MINIMAL_COMPLETION_001.md`.

## 4. Quantum process memory / Markov condition

F. A. Pollock, C. Rodriguez-Rosario, T. Frauenheim, M. Paternostro, K. Modi, *Operational Markov condition for quantum processes*, arXiv:1801.09811.

Cross-check relevance:

- Markovianity is an additional operational property, not automatic for general quantum processes;
- memory/non-Markovianity can be represented without abandoning ordinary quantum theory;
- therefore imposing simple CPTP divisibility as a universal gravity law would be an extra hypothesis, while merely finding process memory would not establish novel gravity.

This supports `AXIOM_AUDIT_H7_PROCESS_COMPOSITION.md`.
