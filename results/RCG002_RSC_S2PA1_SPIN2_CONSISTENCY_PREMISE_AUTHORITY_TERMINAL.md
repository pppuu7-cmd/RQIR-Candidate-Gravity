## P1 — LOCAL ACTION / OFF-SHELL GAUGE DEFORMATION

### External theorem authority

Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri and Henneaux, arXiv:hep-th/0007220, explicitly state their main theorem assumptions: locality, Poincare invariance, Pauli-Fierz free-field action, perturbative gauge-consistent deformation, and at most two derivatives in the Lagrangian. Under those assumptions the consistent deformation is Einstein-Hilbert (or undeformed Pauli-Fierz) sector by sector, modulo field redefinitions.

The same source explicitly notes that when the two-derivative restriction is lifted, higher-derivative gauge-invariant interactions can be constructed. Therefore the derivative-order assumption has real selection power.

Barcelo, Carballo-Rubio and Garay, arXiv:1401.2941, provide an adversarial qualification: self-coupling constructions can display non-uniqueness depending on the starting free formulation and on which gauge symmetry is required to deform/persist; GR/unimodular structures arise only after additional choices.
