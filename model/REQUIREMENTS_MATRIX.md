## Model-selection objective

Among candidates satisfying all frozen design requirements, prefer the construction minimizing unnecessary freedom, schematically

\[
\mathcal C(M)=\alpha N_{\rm free\ parameters}+\beta N_{\rm arbitrary\ functions}+\gamma N_{\rm auxiliary\ assumptions},
\]

subject to the frozen RQIR constraints.

The weights are bookkeeping devices, not fitted physical constants.
