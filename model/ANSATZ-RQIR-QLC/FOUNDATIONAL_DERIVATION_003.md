# Foundational Derivation 003 — ordered response does not by itself escape the Gaussian equivalence class

**Model:** `ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC` v0.1  
**Status:** NEGATIVE / FRONTIER-SETTING RESULT

## 1. Motivation

After CP-saturation was shown not to be gravity-specific, the natural RQIR next step is to include the ordered/commutator sector `D` and retarded response `chi^R` rather than covariance `N` alone.

Frozen RQIR correctly treats this as new operational information: equality of mean and symmetrized noise need not imply equality of ordered response.

The question here is narrower:

> Is the **presence** of a nontrivial ordered/retarded response at L3 sufficient to make the interface new relative to an ordinary quantized mediator/C5?

## 2. Generic Gaussian quantum mediator

Consider a source variable `J` coupled linearly to a quantum mediator/environment degree of freedom. If the mediator sector and initial environment state are Gaussian, integrating out that sector produces a quadratic closed-time-path influence functional of the schematic form

\[
W_{\rm IF}^{(2)}[J_+,J_-]
=
\int J_- D_R J_+
+\frac{i}{2}\int J_- N J_-
\]

up to the declared sum/difference normalization, tensor contractions and factors of `hbar`.

Here:

- `D_R` is the causal retarded response kernel generated by the mediator commutator;
- `N` is its symmetrized fluctuation/noise kernel.

Thus **one ordinary quantum mediator already carries both RQIR L2 and L3 information in one coherent dynamics**.

## 3. Ordered correlators

At second order,

\[
G^>=N+iD,
\qquad
G^<=N-iD,
\]

with the retarded kernel obtained from the commutator sector with causal support.

Therefore a Gaussian unitary dilation is not merely a covariance model. It also fixes the ordered correlators and retarded response of the reduced channel.

Adding `D/chi^R` to the QLC description repairs the information loss of a noise-only representation, but it does not automatically take the model outside the conventional quantum-mediator equivalence class.

## 4. Scoped no-go

Within the linear-Gaussian domain,

\[
\boxed{
\text{nonzero }(N,D,\chi^R)
\not\Rightarrow
\text{new gravity dynamics}
}
\]

and more strongly,

\[
\boxed{
\text{a jointly generated quadratic }(N,D_R)
\text{ sector is compatible with an ordinary quantum mediator}
}
\]

provided a physical dilation exists that also satisfies the gravity-specific gauge, conservation and causal constraints.

The final proviso matters: a model-specific obstruction at the physical gravity level could still distinguish a candidate. This derivation says only that **L3 structure as such** is not the discriminator.

## 5. Relation to RQIR comparator hierarchy

L3 can still be decisive against weaker comparators:

- C1 mean-only semiclassical gravity can fail when equal-mean preparations produce different response;
- C2 classical stochastic models may match `N` yet fail the ordered response or quantum spectral structure;
- restricted C3 hybrids may fail channel-level constraints.

But C5 perturbative quantized gravity is itself a quantum mediator theory in its controlled weak-field regime, so it generically possesses ordered response and quantum noise.

Hence an RQIR-derived **new-theory** discriminator cannot be merely “gravity transmits `D/chi^R`.” It must predict a relation or structure different from the C5 prediction.

## 6. No universal noise-response closure from RQIR alone

Causality, positivity and the operator algebra constrain `N` and `D_R`, but outside special equilibrium/stationary assumptions there is no universal state-independent fluctuation-dissipation equality that lets frozen RQIR uniquely determine the full `N` kernel from `D_R`, or vice versa.

Imposing such a relation globally would therefore be an additional physical postulate unless derived from a declared state/dynamics.

This blocks a tempting but illegitimate escape route: choosing an arbitrary `N=f(D_R)` relation solely to differentiate QLC from C5.

## 7. Surviving frontier

After the two QLC negative results, the generic hierarchy is now:

- L1 mean: insufficient for new-gravity novelty;
- L2 noise: insufficient;
- L3 ordered/retarded response: necessary for some comparator separations, but generically insufficient against a conventional quantum mediator/C5;
- L4 higher connected/non-Gaussian structure: unresolved;
- L5 full process/quantum-information structure: unresolved, but mere entanglement or channel quantumness is already known to be insufficient.

Thus the next honest search target is not “more Gaussian detail” but a **specific L4/L5 closure relation or physical obstruction** forced by the RQIR requirements.

## 8. Decision for ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC

`ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC v0.1` remains a useful operational language, but its generic linear-Gaussian core currently has no established QG-007 discriminator against C5.

Do not add a non-Gaussian term merely to create novelty. First determine whether frozen RQIR leaves the L4/L5 sector underdetermined. If it does, any further closure principle must be declared as an extra hypothesis rather than falsely labeled as derived from RQIR.
