# Foundational Derivation 004 — generic L4/L5 presence is not a novelty criterion

**Model:** `ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC` v0.1  
**Status:** NEGATIVE / SEARCH-SPACE CLOSURE RESULT

## 1. Question

After L1-L3 generic features fail to distinguish QLC from a conventional quantum mediator, can novelty be recovered merely by moving to higher connected cumulants (L4) or quantum-information/process observables (L5)?

## 2. Higher CTP orders

Write the interface influence/generating functional schematically as

\[
W_{\rm IF}=W^{(1)}+W^{(2)}+W^{(3)}+W^{(4)}+\cdots,
\]

where `W^(n)` denotes the n-th connected order in the relevant source/difference variables.

A generic interacting quantum mediator is non-Gaussian. Its microscopic interactions generate nonzero higher connected correlators and nonlinear response vertices,

\[
W^{(n\ge3)}\neq0.
\]

Perturbative quantum gravity/C5 is itself an interacting quantum field theory: graviton self-interactions and matter-gravity interactions generate higher vertices and connected correlators order by order in the EFT expansion.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{W^{(n\ge3)}\neq0\;\not\Rightarrow\;\text{new gravity}}
\]

as a generic criterion.

## 3. L5/process observables

Likewise, the facts that an interface

- can preserve quantum coherence,
- can transmit quantum correlations,
- can generate entanglement,
- has a nontrivial process tensor,
- or has nonclassical channel capacity

are not, by themselves, unique to a new gravity theory. Conventional unitary quantum mediators possess such properties, and frozen RQIR already forbids treating entanglement alone as proof of quantized or novel gravity.

Thus

\[
\boxed{\text{generic process quantumness}\;\not\Rightarrow\;\text{new gravity}}
\]

without a comparator-specific quantitative relation.

## 4. What survives all four negative filters

The independent RQIR construction has now rejected generic novelty claims based on:

1. static density coherence;
2. nonzero covariance/noise;
3. CP-saturating Gaussian added noise;
4. nonzero ordered/retarded response;
5. mere presence of higher connected cumulants;
6. mere quantum-information/process capability.

Therefore a new-theory QG-007 discriminator must have the form

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R[W^{(1)},W^{(2)},W^{(3)},\ldots]=0
}
\]

or an analogous inequality/selection rule, where `R` is a **specific cross-order quantitative relation** that differs from at least one applicable comparator and survives the same gauge, calibration, nuisance and resource treatment.

Alternatively, novelty can arise from a demonstrated **physical obstruction** proving that the C5/C4 realization cannot satisfy the same relational/gauge/conservation/causal requirements in the claimed domain.

## 5. Frozen RQIR does not currently provide that numerical closure relation

RQIR specifies how such a relation must be tested and what consistency properties it must satisfy. It does not, as a methodological framework, assign unique numerical values/functions to every higher CTP vertex.

Choosing, for example,

- `W^(3)=0`,
- a fixed polynomial relation `W^(3)=f(W^(2))`,
- a special nonlocal kernel,
- a collapse strength,
- or a new nonlinear source law

would therefore be an **additional model postulate** unless an independent derivation forces it.

## 6. Consequence for QLC

The generic QLC programme reaches a natural stopping point as a putative new theory:

- its Gaussian core is a valid operational quantum-channel representation;
- ordered-response completion makes it RQIR-complete through L3 but not C5-distinct;
- generic L4/L5 extension does not by itself create novelty;
- a further unique completion cannot honestly be called RQIR-derived without one extra physical principle.

This is not a model failure. It is the point at which RQIR changes role from **deriving admissibility constraints** to **testing independently motivated dynamics**.
