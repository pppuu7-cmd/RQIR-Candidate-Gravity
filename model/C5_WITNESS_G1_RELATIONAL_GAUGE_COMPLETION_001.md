## 2. Diffeomorphism transformation

Under an infinitesimal diffeomorphism generated by `xi^mu`, the perturbative fields transform schematically as

\[
\delta_\xi h_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\xi_\nu+\partial_\nu\xi_\mu+\cdots,
\]

while the coordinate representation of the same physical worldline shifts oppositely,

\[
\delta_\xi z^\mu=-\xi^\mu(z)+\cdots.
\]

Because the line element

\[
ds^2=g_{\mu\nu}dz^\mu dz^\nu
\]

is a scalar geometrical object, the simultaneous transformation of metric and worldline leaves each proper-time functional invariant when its endpoints are physically defined.

Hence

\[
\delta_\xi\tau_A[p,q]=0,
\qquad
\delta_\xi\tau_B[p,q]=0,
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{\delta_\xi\Delta\varphi=0}.
\]

The apparent gauge dependence of a local `Phi(x)` in the weak-field expression is only the coordinate representation of this invariant relational quantity.
