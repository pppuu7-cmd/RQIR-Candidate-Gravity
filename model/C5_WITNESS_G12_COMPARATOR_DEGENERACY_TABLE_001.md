## 1. Audit rule

A signal is not assigned to a microscopic theory merely because one representation can produce it. For each comparator, ask whether the same detector-level probability distribution can be reproduced after allowed state, calibration, nuisance and effective-parameter choices.

The present file audits **degeneracy structure**, not experimental exclusion power.

## 3. Consequences by RQIR layer

### L1 — mean response

C0, C1, C2, C3, C5 and C6 can all reproduce broad classes of mean gravitational response. Mean agreement alone has almost no microscopic resolving power.

### L2 — covariance/noise

C2, C3, C4, C5 and C6 can all generate nontrivial covariance or dephasing. A measured noise floor is not automatically evidence for a quantized gravitational mediator.

### L3 — ordered/retarded response

C5 naturally carries retarded/commutator information, but ordinary quantum mediators and suitable open-system representations can also do so. Generic `D/chi^R` structure is not C5-distinct and not a new-theory discriminator.

### L4 — higher connected functions

Interacting QFT/C5 already generates non-Gaussian connected correlators. Their mere existence does not identify a non-C5 theory. Cross-order quantitative relations must survive the C5 comparator quotient.

### L5 — process/causal structure

Unitary quantum theory already supports nontrivial multi-time process structure. Modified relational composition is not RQIR-forced by current evidence.

## 4. Special role of C5

For the present independent reconstruction, C5 is not just another nuisance model. It is a **witness/root comparator** that already realizes all currently explicit Q1-Q7 constructions and the theory-level consistency gates checked so far.

Therefore any RQIR-only novelty claim must first show

\[
\forall c_5\in C5(D),\qquad P_{candidate}\neq P_{c_5}
\]

after the same observable map and nuisance/calibration quotient.

Existence of even one C5 realization that reproduces the claimed relation blocks C5-distinct QG-007 status.

## 5. What the current audit does and does not prove

It **does** prove that the repository has explicitly considered the main degeneracy classes C0-C6 and that several attractive signatures are non-unique.

It **does not** prove that C0-C4/C6 can reproduce every possible future C5 observable. Such exclusions require observable-specific theorems and detector models.
