# Axiom Audit H7 — relational process composition

**Candidate:** H7 from `NEXT_AXIOM_SEARCH.md`  
**Question:** do frozen RQIR relational/causal requirements force a process-composition law beyond ordinary quantum theory/C5?  
**Result:** NOT RQIR-FORCED at the present level.

## 1. Standard process constraints

For a finite sequence of relationally defined interventions with fixed operational causal order, the multi-time process can be represented by a positive process/comb object `Upsilon` subject to causal normalization constraints of the schematic recursive form

\[
\Upsilon_{n:0}\ge0,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{o_k}\Upsilon_{k:0}
=I_{i_k}\otimes\Upsilon_{k-1:0},
\]

with the exact tensor ordering determined by the chosen Choi/process convention.

These conditions encode probability positivity and the absence of signalling from future choices to past statistics.

## 2. RQIR additions

For gravity, RQIR additionally demands that the interventions/readouts be physically relational/gauge-completed and compatible with the declared causal structure, conservation laws and resource model.

Therefore the admissible process set is not the set of arbitrary laboratory channels: it is the subset whose source/readout maps descend from conserved physical gravity variables.

## 3. Non-uniqueness remains

Positivity plus causal normalization do not select one process tensor. Infinitely many unitary/open-system microscopic dynamics produce different `Upsilon` while satisfying the same composition constraints.

Relational/gauge projection further restricts the admissible set, but it does not by itself assign unique multi-time amplitudes or connected vertices.

A conventional quantum mediator/C5 can itself generate a positive causal multi-time process after the physical gauge sector is treated consistently.

Thus ordinary process consistency is a **gate**, not a new dynamical law.

## 4. Markovian composition is not RQIR-forced

A tempting closure is

\[
\mathfrak I_{A\to C}
=\mathfrak I_{B\to C}\circ\mathfrak I_{A\to B}.
\]

But exact CPTP divisibility is a Markovian assumption. A quantum field theory with unresolved degrees of freedom can generate memory/non-Markovian reduced dynamics without violating fundamental unitarity or causality.

Therefore frozen RQIR does not justify imposing CP divisibility universally.

Conversely, postulating a nonstandard composition law merely to differ from C5 would be an extra hypothesis and risks conflict with ordinary QFT limits.

## 5. Dynamical causal order does not yet provide closure

RQIR Q6 permits testing nonclassical causal/process structure, but permission to test such structure is not a prediction that it must occur.

To turn Q6 into a new model one would need an independent rule specifying the process amplitudes/weights or an obstruction showing that ordinary C5 cannot represent the observed relational process.

No such rule follows from the frozen methodology alone.

## 6. Result

\[
\boxed{
\text{RQIR relational + causal process consistency}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{a unique modified composition law}
}
\]

H7 therefore remains an `EXTRA-HYPOTHESIS` direction rather than an `RQIR-FORCED` extension.

## 7. Scientific consequence

The cheapest process-level escape route does not close the equivalence class. We should not instantiate a modified process law until an independent physical principle or anomaly supplies its form.
