# RQIR-CG-NG-002 — Frozen RQIR does not yet select a unique microscopic dynamics

**Status:** NEGATIVE / RECONSTRUCTION RESULT  
**Scope:** inference from frozen RQIR v1.0 alone, without an extra microscopic closure postulate.

## 1. Question

Does the frozen RQIR funnel, by itself, uniquely determine a new gravity theory once the admissible observable hierarchy, consistency gates, comparators and low-energy limits are imposed?

## 2. What RQIR fixes strongly

RQIR fixes or constrains:

- the operational observable hierarchy;
- baseline/residual discipline;
- the ordered source information `J,N,D/chi^R,...`;
- gauge/relational admissibility;
- conservation/Bianchi/Ward consistency;
- causal support;
- positivity/unitarity/complete positivity as appropriate;
- classical, gravity-off, flat, Newtonian and EFT limits;
- nuisance/calibration identifiability;
- physical resource closure;
- comparator semantics C0-C6;
- the rule that all outputs of a candidate come from one coherent dynamics.

These requirements strongly reduce the admissible model space.

## 3. What RQIR does not presently fix uniquely

Let a candidate influence/generating functional be expanded schematically as

\[
W_{\rm IF}
=
W^{(1)}+W^{(2)}+W^{(3)}+W^{(4)}+\cdots.
\]

The first two levels encode, schematically,

\[
W^{(1)}\leftrightarrow J,
\qquad
W^{(2)}\leftrightarrow (N,D,\chi^R).
\]

Frozen RQIR requires higher connected information to be retained when operationally relevant, but its methodological gates do not currently provide a unique numerical/functional rule fixing every admissible

\[
W^{(n\ge3)}
\]

from `J,N,D/chi^R` alone.

Nor does the framework, by itself, select a unique UV completion, microscopic Hilbert space, nonperturbative state sum, vertex hierarchy, or nonlinear channel law.

## 4. Constructive non-uniqueness

Suppose two candidate microscopic theories share, in a domain `D`,

- the same physical low-energy degrees of freedom;
- the same mean GR transfer;
- the same renormalized `J,N,D/chi^R` through the measured order;
- the same gauge/conservation/causal properties;
- the same detector likelihood after nuisance profiling;

but differ only in higher connected vertices/cumulants outside the measured domain.

Frozen RQIR correctly treats them as operationally degenerate in `D` until an observable sensitive to that difference is introduced.

Therefore RQIR cannot legitimately choose one of them merely because one looks more elegant or because one was designed after seeing comparator failures.

## 5. Result

The current independent reconstruction gives

\[
\boxed{
\text{RQIR v1.0}
\Longrightarrow
\text{a constrained equivalence class of admissible interfaces, not yet a unique new theory}
}
\]

unless an additional physical principle or a new decisive observable closes the remaining functional freedom.

This is consistent with RQIR's own reconstruction objective: a successful outcome may be a sharply constrained equivalence class plus decisive discriminants rather than one microscopic theory.

## 6. Why this is not a failure

The result prevents false model discovery.

The funnel has already removed several illegitimate shortcuts:

1. static density coherence is not enough;
2. nonzero noise is not enough;
3. Gaussian CP saturation is not enough;
4. nonzero ordered response is not enough against a conventional quantum mediator;
5. entanglement alone is not enough.

What remains is a smaller and better-defined search space.

## 7. Consequence for Candidate Gravity construction

Any next model that differs from the C5-like root must identify its added ingredient under one of two labels:

### A. RQIR-forced structure

An additional structure is **RQIR-forced** only if a frozen requirement cannot be represented consistently without it, and the obstruction is demonstrated before the structure is introduced.

### B. Extra physical hypothesis

If multiple structures satisfy all frozen requirements, selecting one is an **extra physical hypothesis**. It may be scientifically valuable, but it is not derived from RQIR and must be labeled separately.

## 8. No-retrofit rule

Comparator performance may falsify or bound a hypothesis, but it may not be used to choose among otherwise-unmotivated higher-order closures and then rebrand the winner as an RQIR derivation.

## 9. Next frontier

The next search is a closure audit over L4/L5:

- higher connected stress/geometry cumulants;
- nonlinear response vertices;
- process tensors / multi-time quantum channels;
- relational causal structure;
- channel composition laws;
- cross-order Ward/positivity identities.

The objective is to ask whether any of these frozen consistency requirements forces a specific cross-order relation not already present in C5.

If not, the scientifically correct RQIR output is the constrained equivalence class plus a list of independently testable extra hypotheses.
