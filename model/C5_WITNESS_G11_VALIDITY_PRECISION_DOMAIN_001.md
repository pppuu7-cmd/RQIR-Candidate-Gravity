# C5 Witness G11 — Low-energy validity and precision-test domain

**Status:** DOMAIN CERTIFICATE  
**Comparator:** C5 perturbative quantum GR/EFT  
**Purpose:** document why the C5 witness need not contradict known precision-GR regimes while making no UV-completion claim.

## 1. EFT domain

The C5 witness is claimed only in a low-energy domain in which the derivative/curvature expansion is controlled. Schematically require

\[
\epsilon_E=\frac{E}{\Lambda_{EFT}}\ll1,
\qquad
\epsilon_R\sim\frac{|R|}{\Lambda_{EFT}^2}\ll1,
\]

with analogous small parameters for higher curvature invariants and momentum transfer.

For the weak-field clock/channel witness additionally require

\[
\frac{|\Phi|}{c^2}\ll1,
\qquad
\frac{v}{c}\ll1
\]

when the slow-motion expansion is used.

The construction makes no claim in trans-Planckian/high-curvature regimes where the low-energy EFT ceases to be controlled.

## 2. Leading theory

At the lowest derivative order the gravitational action is Einstein-Hilbert plus matter,

\[
S_{0}=S_{EH}+S_m,
\]

so the classical leading predictions in the witness domain are those of GR.

Higher-order EFT operators have the schematic form

\[
\Delta S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\sum_i c_i\frac{\mathcal O_i}{\Lambda_i^{d_i-4}},
\]

and are suppressed when the expansion parameters are small, subject to the empirically allowed Wilson-coefficient region.

## 3. Precision-test compatibility rule

G11 does not require that every arbitrary EFT coefficient choice pass experiment. It requires that the claimed model/domain not knowingly contradict tested regimes.

The C5 witness therefore imposes:

1. leading GR normalization fixed by the measured Newton coupling and standard weak-field limit;
2. Wilson coefficients restricted to values compatible with existing bounds in the claimed domain;
3. no extrapolation of the truncated EFT beyond the energy/curvature scale where omitted operators are controlled;
4. any proposed detector signal must be compared to the corresponding precision constraints before novelty is claimed.

Because C5 contains ordinary GR as its leading low-energy limit, a precision-compatible witness region exists.

## 4. Relation to current gravity tests

Modern reviews continue to report strong agreement of GR with Solar-System, binary-pulsar and gravitational-wave tests, including increasingly strong/dynamical regimes. The C5 witness uses this success as a boundary condition rather than attempting to modify it.

Thus any future `EXTRA-HYPOTHESIS` branch that introduces an unsuppressed low-energy deviation would carry a substantially heavier G11 burden than the C5 root.

## 5. Gate effect

Within the explicitly declared domain:

- the leading C5 prediction reduces to tested GR;
- quantum/EFT corrections are organized by power counting;
- unknown high-energy physics is parameterized by higher-order coefficients rather than inserted as uncontrolled low-energy terms;
- the witness is not extended into regimes where its EFT truncation is invalid.

Therefore G11 is **SUPPORTED** for the existence of a precision-compatible C5 witness domain.

This does not mean every UV completion or every arbitrary Wilson-coefficient point is experimentally allowed; the witness proposition requires existence of an allowed C5 realization, not universal viability of every parameter choice.
