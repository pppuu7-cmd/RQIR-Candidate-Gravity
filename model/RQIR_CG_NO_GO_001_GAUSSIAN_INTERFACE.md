# RQIR-CG-NG-001 — Linear-Gaussian interface data do not establish new gravity

**Status:** NEGATIVE / MODEL-SELECTION RESULT  
**Scope:** finite bosonic mode sectors; linear Gaussian CPTP interface; observables restricted to first moments, second moments and linear retarded response.  
**Authority path:** frozen RQIR v1.0 + `ANSATZ-RQIR-QLC/FOUNDATIONAL_DERIVATION_001.md` + `FOUNDATIONAL_DERIVATION_002.md`.

## Statement

Within a finite physical mode sector whose gravity interface is represented by a linear Gaussian quantum channel,

\[
\mathbf d_Y=K\mathbf d_X+\mathbf d_0,
\qquad
V_Y=K V_XK^T+Y,
\]

with

\[
Y+\frac{i\hbar}{2}(\Omega_Y-K\Omega_XK^T)\ge0,
\]

neither

1. nonzero added covariance,
2. saturation of the complete-positivity bound,
3. preservation/transmission of a nontrivial commutator sector, nor
4. the existence of a causal retarded response

is, by itself, sufficient evidence for dynamics beyond a conventional quantum mediator.

## Reason

Any Gaussian CPTP channel has a unitary/symplectic dilation after adjoining a sufficiently large Gaussian environment. Quantum-limited attenuator/amplifier blocks are realized by a pure minimum-uncertainty environment, reproducing the CP-saturating covariance exactly.

Consequently the observable tuple

\[
(K,Y,\Omega_X,\Omega_Y)
\]

can be generated by ordinary unitary quantum dynamics on an enlarged Hilbert space. A gravity model whose only claimed novelty is that it implements such a tuple has not yet distinguished itself from C4/C5-type quantized mediators.

This is an operational non-uniqueness result. It does **not** assert that every mathematically allowed Gaussian channel is realized by perturbative quantum gravity with the same physical source, gauge quotient and apparatus. That stronger statement requires a model-specific C5 calculation.

## Static-sector strengthening

Frozen RQIR-NG-001 further rules out using a density-diagonal static Newtonian readout as a generic coherence discriminator. A constrained Newtonian potential is not to be promoted into an independent canonical oscillator merely to obtain a nonzero CP floor.

Thus the relevant quantum sector, where present, must be a genuine dynamical/relational physical mode sector.

## Comparator consequence

A Gaussian candidate may still distinguish:

- a pure mean-source semiclassical model C1;
- some classical stochastic models C2;
- some restricted hybrid models C3.

But the mere existence of a quantum-limited Gaussian realization does not distinguish it from a conventional quantum mediator or C5 low-energy quantized gravity.

## QG-007 consequence

The following generic novelty criteria are rejected:

- `noise != 0`;
- `CP bound is saturated`;
- `chi^R != 0`;
- `output commutators are nonzero`;
- `Gaussian channel can transmit quantum information`.

A valid QG-007 discriminator must instead be a **specific quantitative relation or higher-order/process prediction** that at least one applicable comparator cannot reproduce in the same physical regime after calibration and nuisance profiling.

## Falsification of this no-go's applicability

This no-go ceases to settle the question when the candidate prediction requires structure outside its assumptions, for example:

- irreducibly non-Gaussian higher connected cumulants;
- nonlinear state-dependent transfer;
- non-Gaussian process tensor constraints;
- a gravity-specific relation among several orders that cannot be represented by a conventional mediator dilation;
- a physical obstruction preventing the C4/C5 dilation from satisfying the same gauge/conservation/causal constraints.

Such a departure must be derived; it may not be inserted solely to evade this result.
