## HYPOTHESIS

Current RCG-002/RSC commitments may or may not contain enough pre-outcome structure to select a physical source constitution after CSA1 showed that a generic covariant action plus Hilbert stress is not unique.

This gate freezes a small candidate set before evaluation. Exactly one survivor would authorize a later source-constitution definition gate. More than one survivor means constitutive ambiguity. Zero survivors means no source constitution principle has been selected.

## REQUIRED OBJECT

A surviving principle must prospectively fix, in one same-realization package:

1. physical probe/apparatus/support/control/binding degrees;
2. branch/history preparation compatible with G97 closure;
3. allowed local curvature/nonminimal couplings;
4. a pre-outcome rule fixing all such coupling coefficients or a proven physical quotient;
5. a local symmetric total source/stress derivation;
6. a carrier nonlinear self-source/action relation;
7. conservation/constraint propagation route;
8. exact recovery of the validated linearized weak-field baseline;
9. no connected-outcome-driven coefficient, source representative, or free nonlinear functional;
10. independence from dynamics imported from external candidate projects.

## FROZEN CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES

### P1 — MGC: Minimal Geometric Coupling

Motivation: the construction contract prioritizes minimal degrees and minimal dynamics. Extend the closed flat-space source constitution to curved spacetime by covariantization only; introduce no explicit curvature-tensor couplings at the defining source scale unless required by an already-frozen physical degree of freedom. The intended role is to fix the CSA1 improvement ambiguity by prospectively forbidding new curvature couplings rather than choosing them from outcomes.

This proposal does not assume that an explicit closed flat-space source action already exists. That missing prerequisite is part of the triage.

### P2 — GML: G97 Mechanical Lift

Motivation: preserve the only explicit closed preparation object already validated. Take the physical source degrees to be exactly the degrees represented in G97's closed probe+apparatus bookkeeping, with no extra hidden source sector. Require a covariant action or constitutive functional whose weak-field/mechanical reduction reproduces the G97 force/impulse relations and whose metric variation defines the total source.

No new mediator field, support continuum, potential, simultaneity rule, or curvature coefficient may be added unless fixed independently of connected outcomes.

### P3 — OSQ: Operational Source Quotient

Motivation: RCG-002 is operational. Treat two local source representatives as physically equivalent only if they generate identical values for all already-validated RCG-002 weak-field operational observables for every admissible closed preparation history. Use this quotient, if nonempty and noncircular, instead of selecting one stress representative by convention.

The quotient must be defined using already-authorized observables and dynamics only. It may not use a future connected `chi_ABC` or an unspecified nonlinear influence law.

### P4 — UVC: Unified Variational Closure

Motivation: RSC seeks one closed nonlinear carrier/source system. Require source and carrier to arise from one local variational/constraint constitution with the existing spin-2 carrier as weak-field limit, at most two derivatives in the classical equations, no new independent dimensionful scales, and no extra carrier/source field introduced solely to select the connected outcome.

The principle must still fix the matter/source ontology and any dimensionless curvature couplings rather than hiding them inside the phrase 'one action'. It may not import Einstein-Hilbert or another external candidate action as the selector.

C1 `RCG002_GROUNDING`: independently motivated by current RCG-002/G97/RSC authority rather than by desired output or external candidate success.

C7 `CONSERVATION_CONSTRAINT_ROUTE`: gives PASS or a concrete defined test route for local conservation/constraint propagation in the same realization.

C9 `NO_FREE_CONNECTED_DATUM`: introduces no free connected coefficient/function/source representative selectable from a connected outcome.

C10 `REPARAMETRIZATION_ROBUSTNESS`: selection is not merely a basis/operator-count convention that changes under admissible field redefinitions, integrations by parts, or equivalent variable choices.
