# RCG-002 RSC AB1 — New Axiom Anchor Budget (Pre-outcome Preregistration)

## Starting question
Does at least one missing model-defining content axiom have independent physical authority strong enough to justify prospective introduction into an RSC successor before any connected outcome is inspected?

## Independence contract
GitHub is the durable scientific authority. Connected outcomes are embargoed: do not compute or use chi_ABC, connected phase/decoherence, preferred nonlinear coefficients, held-out connected observables, or signal size/sign. Do not import nonlinear dynamics, EFT coefficients, vacuum/state choices, or completed candidate architectures from other candidate projects or standard GR/QFT as selectors.

## Anchor taxonomy
A candidate axiom receives credit only from at least one of:

- Type R — an already-authoritative frozen RQIR/RQIRCG physical requirement that *requires* the axiom rather than merely allowing it;
- Type O — an exact obstruction/consistency result showing that without the axiom the physical map is inconsistent, non-conserved, non-covariant, non-positive, acausal, impossible to prepare, or ill-defined;
- Type E — an independently established physical datum/experiment that selects the axiom before any connected RQIRCG outcome.

Words such as minimal, natural, standard, elegant, GR-like, QFT-like, vacuum, no incoming state, least noisy, fewest fields, Occam, renormalizable, familiar, or conventional are motivation only unless converted into an exact physical rule with independent authority.

## Target slots
A. Source ontology/action.
B. Curvature/nonminimal-coupling rule.
C. Nonlinear carrier self-source/constraint law.
D. Quantum state/measure/boundary rule.
E. Operational reduction/causal rule.

## Required candidate record
For each strongest candidate in A–E record: AXIOM_NAME; TARGET_SLOT; EXACT_STATEMENT; MATHEMATICAL_OBJECT; PHYSICAL_CONTENT; ANCHOR_TYPE; AUTHORITY; WHAT_IT_FIXES; WHAT_IT_LEAVES_FREE; COUNTERMODEL_SEARCH; NO_SMUGGLING_TEST; INDEPENDENCE_TEST; VERDICT.

## Selection-power levels
Level I — admissibility only.
Level II — structural correlation/restriction.
Level III — model-definition selection of a physical equivalence class sufficient for the target slot.

Level I/II must not be promoted to Level III.

## Countermodel requirement
For every candidate, search for M1 != M2 satisfying the frozen RQIR requirements, current lower-order RCG-002 structure, G97 closed preparation, the candidate axiom, and all required consistency conditions, while differing in the target model-definition slot. Existence of such a pair means the axiom is at most ADMISSIBILITY_ONLY or PARTIAL_RESTRICTION_ONLY for that slot.

## Three-pass protocol
PASS A — Strongest Constructor: steelman the strongest independently anchored axiom for every slot.
PASS B — Adversarial Falsifier: search for inequivalent realizations, hidden field basis/state/boundary/coefficient/measure freedom, circularity, imported dynamics, and post-hoc motivation.
PASS C — Reconciliation Judge: verdict must be one of INDEPENDENTLY_ANCHORED; PARTIALLY_ANCHORED; MOTIVATED_NOT_SELECTED; INSUFFICIENT_SELECTION_POWER; SMUGGLES_MODEL_CONTENT; BLOCKED_MISSING_AUTHORITY; INVALID.

## Anchor budget
Each slot receives one bookkeeping grade, not a probability:
0 — no anchor;
1 — weak motivation;
2 — structural anchor with meaningful residual freedom;
3 — strong model-defining anchor with sufficient selection power.

## Primary PASS condition
`NEW_AXIOM_ANCHORED_PREOUTCOME_SCOPED` only if at least one content axiom reaches anchor budget 3, with exact independent authority, Level III selection power, residual freedom bounded, no-smuggling passed, and countermodels defeated in the frozen scope.

## Primary negative condition
`RSC_NO_NEW_MODEL_DEFINING_AXIOM_INDEPENDENTLY_ANCHORED_PREOUTCOME_SCOPED` if no candidate reaches budget 3 after Constructor/Falsifier/Judge passes.

## BLOCKED condition
`BLOCKED_ANCHOR_AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` if a candidate plausibly could be Level III but current source authority is insufficient to decide.

## Interpretation ceiling
A PASS only licenses prospective new-version content in the exact target slot; it does not authorize a whole successor theory or chi_ABC. A negative result is a model-definition boundary, not falsification of RQIR or proof that no future independent physical input can select a model.

## Mandatory negative controls
- MAPF1 source calibration: covariant one-action/no-new-scale/flat-matter agreement does not uniquely select stress/source.
- QCPT1/MAPF1 same-unitary-different-state calibration: fixed unitary + pure/positive + exact lower-order recovery does not uniquely select the reduced quantum map.
- Source-side and quantum-side slots remain logically independent unless a separately proven bridge is supplied.

## Claim locks
No NEW_PHYSICS_FOUND; no FULL_QUANTUM_GRAVITY; no THEORY_ESTABLISHED; no RQIR_REQUIRES_RCG002; no universal classical/semiclassical no-go; no family-wide uniqueness; no post-hoc connected-coefficient/state/source selection. Physical selector rank stays `UNDEFINED_PHYSICAL_MAP_MISSING` until a physical map exists. Theory established stays 0% unless repository authority separately changes it.
