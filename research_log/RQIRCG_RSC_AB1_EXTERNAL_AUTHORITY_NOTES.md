## EA1 — massless spin-2 consistent-deformation theorem

SOURCE: N. Boulanger, T. Damour, L. Gualtieri, M. Henneaux, *Inconsistency of interacting, multi-graviton theories*, Nucl. Phys. B597 (2001) 127–171.

VERSION: arXiv:hep-th/0007220v2 (5 Oct 2000), https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0007220 .

EXACT STATEMENT USED: for a finite collection of massless spin-2 fields whose free limit is a sum of Pauli–Fierz actions, under the paper's local perturbative gauge-deformation framework and interactions with at most two derivatives, the allowed deformations do not produce Yang–Mills-like cross-couplings; the possible deformations are individual Einstein–Hilbert sectors. In the single-carrier use here, the relevant content is the strong restriction placed on local two-derivative gauge-consistent Pauli–Fierz deformations.

SCOPE: local Lagrangian/action deformation; Pauli–Fierz free action; Poincaré background/free limit; perturbative gauge consistency; at most two derivatives. It is not an assumption-free theorem that every massless spin-2 physical theory must be Einstein gravity.

USE IN RQIRCG: Type-O theorem source for slot C (nonlinear carrier self-source/constraint). It supports a strong *conditional structural anchor* if RQIRCG independently fixes all theorem premises.

WHY THIS IS NOT IMPORTED DYNAMICS: AB1 does not adopt the Einstein–Hilbert action. It audits whether the theorem's premises are already RQIRCG authority. They are not all fixed: current RCG-002 has a linearized spin-2 equation/gauge law but no selected local carrier action/source constitution. Therefore the theorem is not promoted to a model selector.

## EA2 — self-coupling representation counterexample/control

SOURCE: C. Barceló, R. Carballo-Rubio, L. J. Garay, *Unimodular gravity and general relativity from graviton self-interactions*.

VERSION: arXiv:1401.2941, https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.2941 .

EXACT STATEMENT USED: the authors explicitly find an entire family in a graviton self-coupling construction and show that imposing the appropriate deformed gauge invariance leads to unimodular-gravity or general-relativity structures depending on the starting free formulation.

SCOPE: a specific flat-background self-interaction construction; its assumptions and off-shell formulation matter.

USE IN RQIRCG: falsifier control for slot C. It demonstrates that the slogan `massless spin-2 self-couples` is not by itself a representation-independent unique selector.

WHY THIS IS NOT IMPORTED DYNAMICS: neither unimodular gravity nor GR is adopted. The result is used only to test overclaim and hidden off-shell/gauge-formulation assumptions.

## EA3 — Weak Equivalence Principle datum

SOURCE: P. Touboul et al., *MICROSCOPE mission: final results of the test of the Equivalence Principle*.

VERSION: arXiv:2209.15487, 30 Sep 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15487 .

EXACT STATEMENT USED: the Ti/Pt differential free-fall test found no WEP violation, with Eötvös parameter `eta(Ti,Pt)=[-1.5 ± 2.3(stat) ± 1.5(syst)]×10^-15` at 1-sigma as reported by the collaboration.

SCOPE: composition-dependent free fall of the tested macroscopic materials in the MICROSCOPE orbital weak-field regime. It does not directly measure arbitrary local curvature operators in an unspecified microscopic RCG-002 source ontology.

USE IN RQIRCG: Type-E empirical restriction for slot B. Any future source/coupling constitution mapping to composition-dependent free fall must respect this bound in its domain.

WHY THIS IS NOT IMPORTED DYNAMICS: only the experimental null constraint is used. No GR matter action or EFT coefficient is selected.

## EA4 — no generally covariant preferred-state choice

SOURCE: C. J. Fewster, R. Verch, *Dynamical locality and covariance: What makes a physical theory the same in all spacetimes?*, Ann. Henri Poincaré 13 (2012) 1613–1674.

VERSION: arXiv:1106.4785v3, 8 May 2012, https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4785 .

EXACT STATEMENT USED: within the paper's locally covariant framework, dynamically local theories satisfying the stated typical assumptions admit a model-independent no-go result against making a covariant choice of preferred state in all spacetimes.

SCOPE: locally covariant QFT framework and the theorem's dynamical-locality/typical assumptions. This is not a theorem that a specific future RSC realization can never have a distinguished state after extra physical boundary structure is supplied.

USE IN RQIRCG: falsifier for slot D. It blocks the unqualified rule `choose the generally covariant vacuum` from receiving Level-III selection credit.

WHY THIS IS NOT IMPORTED DYNAMICS: the theorem is used only to constrain state-selection logic.

## EA5 — Hadamard admissibility/finite-fluctuation motivation

SOURCE: C. J. Fewster, R. Verch, *The Necessity of the Hadamard Condition*.

VERSION: arXiv:1307.5242, 19 Jul 2013, https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.5242 .

EXACT STATEMENT USED: in the paper's ultrastatic-slab setting with compact Cauchy surface, finiteness of fluctuations of Wick squares of all time derivatives forces Hadamard behavior under the stated construction; the work supplies an exact physical/mathematical motivation for Hadamard admissibility.

SCOPE: QFT state admissibility/singularity structure in the specified field/spacetime setting. It does not select one member of the Hadamard state space.

USE IN RQIRCG: Type-O structural anchor for slot D: a future curved-spacetime quantum constitution should have a state admissibility condition strong enough to support well-defined local observables. It is not a unique-state rule.

WHY THIS IS NOT IMPORTED DYNAMICS: no external QFT vacuum or Hamiltonian is adopted; only the admissibility theorem is used.
