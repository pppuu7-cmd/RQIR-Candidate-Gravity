## P1 — local action / off-shell gauge deformation (`LAG2`)

### Primary theorem source

N. Boulanger, T. Damour, L. Gualtieri, M. Henneaux,
"Inconsistency of interacting, multi-graviton theories",
arXiv:hep-th/0007220; Nuclear Physics B 597 (2001) 127-171.
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0007220

The paper states its main theorem assumptions explicitly. The relevant theorem class assumes:

- locality;
- Poincare invariance;
- a free field limit given by Pauli-Fierz action(s);
- perturbative gauge-consistent deformation of an action;
- at most two derivatives in the interacting Lagrangian for the Einstein-Hilbert uniqueness theorem;
- equivalence modulo field redefinitions.

Within those assumptions the consistent deformation is Einstein-Hilbert (or undeformed Pauli-Fierz) sector by sector.

Crucial qualification from the same source: if the `<=2 derivative` assumption is removed, higher-derivative gauge-invariant interactions built from linearized curvatures are available. Thus the derivative restriction has genuine selection content and cannot be treated as a harmless notation convention.

### Qualification/counterweight

C. Barcelo, R. Carballo-Rubio, L. J. Garay,
"Unimodular gravity and general relativity from graviton self-interactions",
arXiv:1401.2941; Phys. Rev. D 89, 124019 (2014).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.2941

This analysis makes explicit that self-coupling iterations can exhibit non-uniqueness depending on the starting free formulation and on which gauge symmetry is required to deform/persist; the paper obtains unimodular/GR-like structures under additional gauge-preservation requirements. It is used here only as an adversarial warning that the starting formulation and gauge-deformation premise are substantive.

Not parent-authorized:

### Verdict

P1 has genuine overlap with the parent weak-field baseline, but at least the action/deformation-class premise and nonlinear derivative-order restriction remain new model-defining assumptions. The theorem therefore cannot supply authority for its own applicability.

## P2 — asymptotic soft/S-matrix route (`SFT2`)

### Primary soft source

S. Weinberg,
"Photons and Gravitons in S-Matrix Theory: Derivation of Charge Conservation and Equality of Gravitational and Inertial Mass",
Phys. Rev. 135, B1049 (1964), DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.135.B1049.
https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.135.B1049

Weinberg states the operative assumptions as Lorentz invariance and pole structure of the S-matrix plus massless spin-1/spin-2 particle content. The result gives the soft-graviton universality/equality of gravitational and inertial mass and a mass-shell gauge-invariance condition. It is a strong universal-coupling constraint, but it is not by itself a complete nonlinear Einstein-action reconstruction.

### Stronger constructible-S-matrix source

P. Benincasa, F. Cachazo,
"Consistency Conditions on the S-Matrix of Massless Particles",
arXiv:0705.4305.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.4305

Their consistency tests apply to four-dimensional Minkowski theories whose four-particle amplitudes are obtained from three-particle amplitudes by BCFW construction; they call this class `constructible`. In that class they derive strong restrictions including spin-2 uniqueness statements.

### Parent-to-theorem map

Parent-authorized or aligned:

- nearly Minkowski weak-field baseline;
- Lorentz/covariant aspirations;
- massless-spin-2 interpretation of the linearized comparator;
- no-superluminal operational requirement.

Not parent-authorized:

- a physical asymptotic particle/S-matrix ontology for the RCG-002 carrier;
- existence of asymptotic in/out graviton states for the relevant closed relational laboratory realization;
- S-matrix pole/factorization assumptions as model axioms;
- BCFW constructibility or equivalent large-complex-momentum behavior;
- analyticity/factorization conditions sufficient for complete tree-level reconstruction;
- a theorem that maps soft universal coupling alone to the full missing RSC nonlinear carrier law.

The operational channel seed and retarded finite-time baseline can be formulated without assuming an asymptotic S-matrix. No current parent rule upgrades `no superluminal signalling` or covariance to S-matrix constructibility.

### Verdict

`NOT_FORCED_BY_CURRENT_RQIRCG`.

The soft theorem is a legitimate conditional Type-O/structural result once its scattering premises hold, but those premises are not selected by current parent authority. The stronger uniqueness route adds constructibility assumptions that are also absent.
