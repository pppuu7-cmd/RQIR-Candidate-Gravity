# Iter035 / G47-P — three-state hidden-classical switching provenance

## Purpose
Response-blind implementation/provenance pre-gate for a classical finite-memory comparator family that is distinct from the previously unresolved two-state RTN adversarial branch.

No RCG-002 target is used. This gate validates only that the implemented reduced channel is generated by an explicit three-state classical continuous-time Markov switching process whose conditional Hamiltonian is always a sum of local terms, so every microscopic trajectory is product-unitary.

## Frozen construction
Hidden state `j in {0,1,2}` follows a continuous-time Markov chain with nonnegative transition rates and column-sum-zero generator `Q`. Conditional quantum Hamiltonians are
`H_j = hA_j·sigma_A ⊗ I + I ⊗ hB_j·sigma_B`.
Quantum-classical blocks obey
`d rho_j/dt = -i[H_j,rho_j] + sum_k Q[j,k] rho_k`.
The visible state is `rho(t)=sum_j rho_j(t)` with the hidden chain initialized in its stationary distribution.

Six deterministic response-blind shards are frozen before production. Seeds/parameter formulas are fixed in the implementation and do not inspect candidate-target data.

## Frozen diagnostics
For every shard:
- all off-diagonal transition rates `>=0` and column sums of `Q` have absolute residual `<=1e-12`;
- stationary distribution is nonnegative, normalized to `1` within `1e-12`, and `||Q pi||<=1e-10`;
- exact local-sum factorization check for every conditional propagator at `dt in {0.07,0.19}`: `||exp(-i H_j dt) - exp(-i hA_j·sigma dt)⊗exp(-i hB_j·sigma dt)||_F <=1e-11`;
- visible channel at `t in {0.15,0.45,0.9,1.4}` is trace preserving within `1e-10` and has minimum Choi eigenvalue `>=-1e-8`;
- output negativity for 16 deterministic product inputs at `t=0.9` is `<=1e-9`;
- all diagnostics finite.

A reduced-channel non-semigroup witness is evaluated prospectively at `(t,s)=(0.45,0.45)` using Frobenius superoperator defect `||E(t+s)-E(t)E(s)||_F`. At least four of six frozen shards must have defect `>1e-4`. This is a finite-memory/non-semigroup witness only; BLP information backflow is not required and no general non-Markovian theorem is claimed.

## Aggregate classification
PASS iff all six shards satisfy structural/classical-provenance conditions and at least four satisfy the frozen non-semigroup witness:
`THREE_STATE_CLASSICAL_SWITCHING_PRODUCT_UNITARY_PROVENANCE_VALIDATED`.

If classical provenance/CP/TP/factorization fails, classify implementation/structural failure. If provenance passes but fewer than four non-semigroup witnesses pass, classify
`THREE_STATE_CLASSICAL_SWITCHING_MEMORY_WITNESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
