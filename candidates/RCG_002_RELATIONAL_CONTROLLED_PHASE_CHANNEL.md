# RCG-002 — Relational controlled-phase channel

Status: `SEED / PREREGISTERED COMPARATOR TEST`
Date: 2026-09-12

## Derivation rule

RCG-002 is derived only from the frozen RQIR-CG construction contract and the Iter001 result that the Gaussian response/noise layer is exactly degenerate with a classical stochastic model in the tested domain.

No QGR candidate structure is imported.

## Minimal operational extension

Take two relationally addressed two-level probes `A,B`, initially separable. The smallest coherence-sensitive nonlocal channel extension is

`rho -> E_eta( U_chi rho U_chi^dagger )`,

with

`U_chi = diag(1,1,1,exp(i chi))`

in the relational product basis and `E_eta` a local dephasing channel used only for robustness tests.

`chi` is an operational relational phase. At this stage no microscopic formula for `chi` is asserted. A future candidate must derive its source, distance, time, `G`, and `hbar` dependence rather than fit it freely.

The Gaussian RCG-001 response layer may coexist as a baseline nuisance layer, but it cannot by itself supply the quantum-specific witness.

## Frozen comparator quotient

Before any RCG-002 computation, freeze the classical comparator class as arbitrary correlated mixtures of local phase unitaries

`rho -> sum_lambda p_lambda [U_A(lambda) x U_B(lambda)] rho [U_A(lambda) x U_B(lambda)]^dagger`.

This is the operational class generated by shared classical stochastic phase noise without a nonlocal quantum interaction. It maps separable inputs to separable outputs and therefore has zero entanglement negativity for the frozen product input.

This comparator class is deliberately scoped. RCG-002 does **not** rule out every conceivable classical mediator model, measurement-and-feedback architecture, or hidden-variable completion.

## Frozen Iter002 gates

G7 — channel consistency: verify exact unitarity/CPTP normalization for `U_chi` and trace preservation after local dephasing.

G8 — comparator-resistant witness: from `|++><++|`, compute partial-transpose negativity of the RCG-002 output and compare against a Monte-Carlo ensemble of arbitrarily correlated local phase unitaries. Promotion requires positive RCG-002 negativity and comparator negativity <= numerical tolerance.

G9 — parameter-free holdout identity: for the noiseless branch and held-out `chi` values not used in any fit, verify

`Negativity(|psi_chi>) = |sin(chi/2)| / 2`.

This is a cross-check tying the relational phase channel to an independently evaluated entanglement observable; it is an internal holdout relation, not external experimental evidence.

G10 — dephasing robustness: freeze `eta in {1.0,0.95,0.85,0.70,0.50}` and map where the entanglement witness survives without changing thresholds after inspection.

G11 — zero-interaction null: `chi=0` must produce zero negativity and identity coherent action.

## Promotion rule

Passing G7-G11 permits at most

`DISTINGUISHABLE_SCOPED_FROM_CORRELATED_LOCAL_PHASE_STOCHASTIC_COMPARATOR`.

It does not permit `NEW_PHYSICS`, `FULL_QUANTUM_GRAVITY`, or a claim that gravity mediates entanglement in nature.

## Microscopic blockers

- derive `chi` rather than postulate it;
- embed the channel in a causal relativistic response construction;
- show compatibility with the RCG-001 limits and covariance layer;
- derive a multi-channel source/geometry map;
- confront semiclassical-gravity and classical-channel comparators broader than correlated local phases;
- obtain an externally meaningful held-out observable.
