# Wave 22 Holdout Design Intent

The next stage is a prospective, candidate-blind discriminator bank for any future polygon-derived QGR candidate. It will be designed before candidate equations are imported.

Planned independent axes: tensor-sector visibility, cross-order 2->3 point sensitivity, pole/residue consistency, finite-kinematic higher-point visibility, four-point inequality information, nuisance/calibration robustness, and full-rank identifiability across the residual action directions left by Wave 20.

No KMQGB candidate equations, ansatz or architecture may be used in Wave 22. Only the frozen RQIR-v1 endpoint and established external physics constraints are admissible.
