# Wave 19 — Cross-Order Closure Pre-Registration

**Branch:** `cross-order-closure-wave19`  
**Parent:** Wave 18 correction commit.  
**Authority:** frozen RQIR v1.0; no polygon-QGR architecture.

## Question

Can gravity consistency principles derive physical higher-point dynamics from the already constrained two-point transverse kernel, or does C5/EFT retain independent cubic interaction directions?

## Predeclared tests

1. **Ward cross-order rank:** decompose a reduced 3-point response into longitudinal and transverse basis directions; measure which components are fixed by two-point data through a Ward-type identity.
2. **Soft-contact freedom:** test corrections that vanish through first subleading soft order but alter finite kinematics.
3. **Cubic-only EFT control:** perturbative degree counting for a curvature-cubed direction that begins at `O(h^3)` and therefore changes a three-graviton vertex without changing the leading quadratic kernel.
4. **Causality inequality:** test whether a positive-delay-style admissibility bound selects a unique cubic coefficient or only an interval.
5. **Strong same-generating product law:** deliberately impose a restrictive cross-order product rule using the same Wave-18 exponential dressing and ask whether it produces parameter-free 3-point predictions. This is a selector-strength demonstration, not a novelty claim.
6. **Prospective cross-order holdout:** construct candidates identical in selected two-point, Ward-longitudinal and leading/subleading-soft data but differing in an untouched finite 3-point observable.
7. **Comparator firewall.**

## Fail-closed semantics

Ward, soft and causality constraints count as a unique cross-order derivation only if no physical transverse cubic/contact direction survives. A C5/EFT cubic-only control is sufficient to block uniqueness.

A strong product rule may count as *predictive inside its restricted class*, but cannot be promoted unless independently derived from gravity and shown distinct from C5/EFT after frozen RQIR validation.

No Wave-19 relation may be added or adjusted after seeing the finite 3-point holdout.
