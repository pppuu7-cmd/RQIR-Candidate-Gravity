# Two-Point Escape Triangle — Post-Freeze Design Constraint

**Status:** SYNTHESIS OF WAVES 2-3 / NOT A COMPLETE QUANTUM-GRAVITY THEOREM

## 1. Baseline assumptions

Start from the physical TT sector with:

- a unit-residue massless GR graviton pole;
- ordinary positive-norm/spectral interpretation;
- standard retarded/causal response semantics;
- no additional low-energy propagating state unless explicitly declared.

Wave 2 and Wave 3 show that a nontrivial two-point modification cannot be inserted for free under these assumptions.

## 2. Route A — add positive spectral support

Keep the standard positive spectral representation.

Then any two-point deviation above the pure GR pole is represented by additional positive spectral support:

`rho_extra != 0`.

Physical price:

- new state(s), resonance(s), continuum or equivalent extra spectral content;
- new masses/couplings/spectral law;
- precision and production/decay constraints;
- RQIR must propagate those degrees of freedom through `J,N,D,chiR,...`.

Wave 2 shows finitely many low-energy moments do not uniquely determine that spectral sector.

## 3. Route B — keep no new positive states but change spectral/analytic structure

Example proxy: exponential entire UV softening

`exp(-ell^2 Q^2)/Q^2`.

With the same unit GR pole this lies below `1/Q^2` for Euclidean `Q^2>0`, while adding nonnegative spectral weight only raises the standard Stieltjes/KL propagator above `1/Q^2`.

Physical price:

- abandon or modify at least one standard positive-spectral assumption;
- provide a new microscopic meaning for the form factor;
- re-certify unitarity/positivity and the CTP response hierarchy.

## 4. Route C — modify the pole prescription

A nonstandard prescription may remove a standard ghost interpretation without adding an asymptotic particle in the usual way.

Physical price:

- the prescription itself becomes new dynamical structure;
- its retarded/causal support and operational no-signalling semantics must be established;
- time-symmetric proxies demonstrate that pole reinterpretation can carry negative-time response support.

Thus a modified prescription is not merely a relabeling of the same propagator under RQIR Q6/G4.

## 5. Escape outside the triangle — do not modify the two-point function

A fourth logically different option is to keep the GR propagator and modify only higher-point interactions.

Curvature-cubic and higher operators provide explicit examples: they start beyond quadratic order.

But ordinary gravitational EFT/C5 already contains this freedom. Therefore a higher-point-only Candidate Gravity needs an extra principle that **fixes a comparator-orthogonal relation among higher-point coefficients/amplitudes**, not merely a nonzero higher-curvature term.

## 6. Design consequence

For the next Candidate Gravity search, every proposed C5-distinct mechanism must declare which cost it pays:

| Route | New states? | Standard positive spectrum? | Standard retarded semantics? | Two-point modified? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A positive spectral support | yes | yes | potentially yes | yes |
| B nonstandard/nonlocal analytic structure | not necessarily | no / modified | must re-prove | yes |
| C modified pole prescription | not necessarily asymptotic | modified interpretation | nontrivial burden | yes |
| D higher-point-only | no at quadratic level | yes at two-point | yes at two-point | no |

Any future ansatz that hides this choice is incomplete under RQIR.
