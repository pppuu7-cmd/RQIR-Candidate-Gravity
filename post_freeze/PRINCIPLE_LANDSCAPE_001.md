# Post-Freeze Principle Landscape 001

**Status:** EXPLORATORY AUDIT / NO EXTRA HYPOTHESIS ADOPTED  
**Purpose:** search for independently motivated physical principles after the RQIR-only v0 freeze, without selecting a law because it beats C5 or the KMQGB polygon.

## 1. Admission rule

A post-freeze principle is not promoted merely because it is interesting or C5-distinct. To become a serious candidate seed it must satisfy prospectively:

1. independent physical motivation predating comparator performance;
2. finite primitive data / bounded functional freedom;
3. covariance/gauge and conservation consistency;
4. causal and unitary/positive/CP consistency as applicable;
5. controlled GR, QM and low-energy limits;
6. a concrete prediction fixed before detector/comparator tuning;
7. an explicit C5-difference direction if novelty is claimed;
8. a falsification condition;
9. no immediate reduction to an already registered comparator family.

Classifications used below:

- `RETURNS_TO_C5`;
- `KNOWN_COMPARATOR`;
- `CONSTRAINS_BUT_DOES_NOT_CLOSE`;
- `HIGH_COST_EXTRA_HYPOTHESIS`;
- `CANDIDATE_SEED`.

## 2. P1 — quantum equivalence principle / quantum reference frames

Independent motivation: extend covariance/equivalence principles to quantum reference systems and superposed gravitational situations.

Representative literature:

- Zych & Brukner, *Quantum formulation of the Einstein equivalence principle*, Nature Physics 14, 1027 (2018);
- Giacomini, Castro-Ruiz & Brukner, *Quantum mechanics and the covariance of physical laws in quantum reference frames*, Nature Communications 10, 494 (2019);
- Giacomini & Brukner, arXiv:2012.13754 and arXiv:2109.01405.

Positive value:

- directly aligned with RQIR Q1/G1 relationality;
- supplies a principled quantum-covariance requirement;
- disfavors naive claims that a mass superposition by itself requires collapse.

Closure failure:

- covariance under quantum-frame changes does not by itself select a unique microscopic gravity Hamiltonian/action/process;
- the principle is compatible with ordinary unitary quantum descriptions of superposed gravitational configurations;
- no generic C5 obstruction follows.

Classification:

`CONSTRAINS_BUT_DOES_NOT_CLOSE`.

No new-model promotion.

## 3. P2 — entanglement equilibrium / causal-diamond thermodynamic selection

Independent motivation: local vacuum entanglement stationarity/maximality as a physical principle.

Representative literature:

- Jacobson, *Entanglement Equilibrium and the Einstein Equation*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 201101 (2016), arXiv:1505.04753.

Positive value:

- nontrivial connection between quantum-information structure and Einstein dynamics;
- potentially relevant to a relational/state-based parent law.

Closure/novelty result:

- in its established regime the principle recovers the Einstein equation (with assumptions/conjectures for matter sectors) rather than selecting a C5-distinct microdynamics;
- it is therefore valuable structural support for the GR root but not a new quantum-gravity discriminator.

Classification:

`RETURNS_TO_C5` at the leading gravitational-dynamics level.

No new-model promotion.

## 4. P3 — causality / consistency of an interacting massless spin-2 field

Independent motivation: causal Lorentz-invariant propagation and consistency of spin-2 interactions.

Representative literature:

- Hertzberg & Sandora, *General Relativity from Causality*, arXiv:1702.07720;
- broader soft-graviton/gauge-consistency literature underlying universal gravitational coupling.

Positive value:

- strong restriction on competing spin-2 interactions;
- reinforces the special status of single-species GR-like low-energy dynamics.

Novelty result:

- the direction of the constraint is toward GR rather than away from C5;
- higher-derivative possibilities require additional structure and are already EFT/comparator territory.

Classification:

`RETURNS_TO_C5` / strengthens the low-energy root.

No new-model promotion.

## 5. P4 — fundamental classical gravity with completely positive classical–quantum dynamics

Independent motivation: ask whether spacetime can remain fundamentally classical while matter is quantum without inconsistent probabilities.

Representative literature:

- Oppenheim et al., *Gravitationally induced decoherence vs space-time diffusion: testing the quantum nature of gravity*, Nature Communications 14, 7910 (2023), arXiv:2203.01982.

Positive value:

- gives a quantitative decoherence–diffusion tradeoff for broad Markovian classical–quantum dynamics;
- supplies falsifiable experimental signatures and a finite structural inequality rather than a vague slogan.

Novelty result:

- this is precisely a C3 classical-channel/hybrid/postquantum comparator direction already present in RQIR;
- it may compete with C5 experimentally but is not a novel model produced by the present programme.

Classification:

`KNOWN_COMPARATOR (C3)`.

Do not rename as Candidate Gravity novelty.

## 6. P5 — gravity-related spontaneous collapse / Diósi–Penrose direction

Independent motivation: objective state reduction linked to gravitational self-energy / incompatibility of macroscopic superpositions.

Representative current constraints:

- Donadi et al., *Underground test of gravity-related wave function collapse*, Nature Physics 17, 74 (2021): the natural parameter-free DP version was excluded by the reported radiation bound;
- Figurato et al., arXiv:2406.18494: current viable DP descriptions retain a regularization scale and only a window of cutoff values survives phenomenological requirements;
- broader collapse-model experiments continue to constrain the parameter space.

Positive value:

- genuinely C5-distinct in principle because it modifies unitary quantum dynamics;
- experimentally falsifiable.

Costs/problems:

- requires a regulator/cutoff or equivalent microscopic structure;
- natural parameter-free realization has already encountered direct experimental exclusion;
- relativistic covariance, conservation/heating and microscopic completion carry a high burden;
- the idea is an established collapse-model family, not a new architecture originating here.

Classification:

`HIGH_COST_EXTRA_HYPOTHESIS / KNOWN COLLAPSE FAMILY`.

Not adopted.

## 7. First landscape result

The low-cost independently motivated principles examined so far divide into two groups:

\[
\text{strong consistency principles}\rightarrow\text{GR/C5-like root},
\]

or

\[
\text{C5-distinct principles}\rightarrow\text{known comparator / extra dynamics with new parameters or structures}.
\]

No principle in this first scan simultaneously provides

- independent motivation,
- finite/rigid microscopic closure,
- covariance/causality/positivity control,
- and a genuinely new C5-distinct prediction.

Therefore **no EXTRA-HYPOTHESIS is promoted in this iteration**.

## 8. Implication for model construction

The result sharpens the earlier no-go rather than ending the search:

> the missing ingredient is not merely 'one more consistency condition'. Strong low-energy consistency conditions tend to reconstruct GR/C5. To obtain a distinct microscopic theory without retrofit, the programme needs a genuinely new physical primitive whose observable consequence is fixed before comparator testing.

## 9. Next search classes

Continue only in directions capable of supplying a finite upstream primitive rather than another conditional map:

1. a finite information/state-selection law that fixes both state space and dynamics rather than only constraining them;
2. a spectral/discrete primitive with a unique continuum and CTP map, but only if it does not reduce to an existing spectral/causal-set/tensor comparator;
3. an empirical anomaly/residual that forces a new operator or process term;
4. a new symmetry/algebra with a demonstrable anomaly-free representation and a pre-fixed observable consequence.

Each must first pass the known-comparator identity test before any equation is added to a Candidate Gravity ansatz.
